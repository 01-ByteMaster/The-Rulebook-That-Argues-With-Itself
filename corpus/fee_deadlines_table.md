# Fee Deadlines and Financial Calendar — Medicaps University

This document consolidates all fee-related deadlines, late-fee schedules, and refund windows applicable to undergraduate and postgraduate students for the current academic year. It is maintained by the Accounts Office and updated at the start of each academic session.

## FEE-1: Semester Fee Due Dates
<!-- section_id: FEE-1 | conflict_group: null -->

Tuition and associated fees for each semester are due according to the following schedule:

| Semester      | Fee Payment Window Opens | Fee Payment Deadline | Late Fee Begins |
|---------------|--------------------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| Odd Semester  | 1st June                 | 30th June            | 1st July        |
| Even Semester | 1st December             | 31st December        | 1st January     |

Students must complete payment through the university's online payment portal or at the Accounts Office counter during working hours (9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Friday). Payments received after the deadline but before the late fee cutoff described in FEE-2 are accepted subject to the applicable late fee surcharge. Receipts are generated electronically and available on the student portal within 24 hours of a successful transaction.

## FEE-2: Late Fee Schedule
<!-- section_id: FEE-2 | conflict_group: null -->

Students who do not pay the full semester fee by the published deadline are subject to the following graduated late fee surcharge:

| Period After Deadline | Late Fee Surcharge |
|-----------------------|--------------------|
| 1–7 days              | ₹500 flat          |
| 8–14 days             | ₹1,000 flat        |
| 15–30 days            | ₹2,500 flat        |
| Beyond 30 days        | Registration hold; student must meet the Accounts Officer in person to resolve |

Late fees are non-refundable and are charged in addition to the full semester fee, not as a replacement for any portion of it. A registration hold imposed after the 30-day window prevents the student from accessing examination registration, hostel allotment for the following semester, and official transcript requests until the outstanding balance is cleared. The Accounts Office may waive late fees in exceptional circumstances (documented medical emergency, natural disaster affecting the student's home region, or similar) upon written application, but such waivers are granted at the discretion of the Chief Accounts Officer and are not automatic.

## FEE-3: Fee Structure Components
<!-- section_id: FEE-3 | conflict_group: null -->

The total semester fee comprises the following components, each billed as a separate line item on the fee statement so that students can identify the cost breakdown:

| Component              | Approximate Range (per semester) | Notes                                        |
|------------------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Tuition Fee            | ₹35,000 – ₹75,000               | Varies by program and year of study          |
| Laboratory/Studio Fee  | ₹5,000 – ₹15,000                | Applicable only to programs with lab/studio  |
| Hostel Fee             | ₹20,000 – ₹30,000               | Applicable only to hostel residents          |
| Mess Fee               | ₹15,000 – ₹18,000               | Applicable only to students on mess plan     |
| Student Activity Fee   | ₹2,000                           | Flat rate, all students                      |
| Examination Fee        | ₹1,500                           | Per semester, covers end-semester exams       |
| Technology & Library   | ₹3,000                           | Portal access, library resources, IT support |

Individual component amounts are set by the Fee Revision Committee at the start of each academic year and published on the university website along with the fee payment schedule. Students on scholarship (see Scholarship Policy) have the applicable scholarship amount adjusted against the tuition and hostel components first, with any remaining scholarship credit applied to other components in the order listed above.

## FEE-4: Tuition Refund Policy for Course or Program Withdrawal
<!-- section_id: FEE-4 | conflict_group: null -->

Students who withdraw from a course or from the university entirely after paying the semester fee are entitled to a tuition refund according to the following tiered schedule, calculated from the official start date of the semester:

| Withdrawal Period            | Refund Percentage |
|------------------------------|-------------------|
| Within weeks 1–2             | 100% of tuition   |
| During weeks 3–6             | 50% of tuition    |
| After week 6                 | No refund          |

This refund applies to the tuition component only; the examination fee, student activity fee, and technology & library fee are non-refundable after the first day of the semester. Hostel and mess fee refunds follow their own schedules as described in the Hostel Handbook and this document respectively. A student who withdraws and later seeks re-admission in a subsequent semester must pay the full fee applicable at the time of re-admission, regardless of any prior refund received. Refunds are processed within 20 working days of the formal withdrawal being recorded by the Registrar's office and are credited to the student's bank account on file.

## FEE-5-REFUND-B: Monthly Refund Processing Cutoff — Including Hostel Deposit Refunds
<!-- section_id: FEE-5-REFUND-B | conflict_group: CG-HOSTEL-REFUND -->

All refund requests — including hostel security deposit refunds for students who vacate or leave the hostel early, tuition refunds for withdrawal, and mess fee adjustments — are processed in a single monthly batch by the Accounts Office. The cutoff date for inclusion in a given month's refund batch is the 10th of that month; any hostel deposit refund request received or completed (including all required sign-offs such as the no-dues certificate from the Hostel Warden) after the 10th is automatically rolled into the following month's batch. No refunds of any kind — whether hostel deposit, security deposit, or other hostel-related refund — are processed after the 10th of the month in which the student vacates the hostel or withdraws, regardless of the exit date or the date on which supporting paperwork was submitted. This batching system means that the actual calendar time between a student leaving the hostel early and receiving their deposit back depends significantly on when in the month the departure occurs: a student who completes the hostel checkout and all no-dues paperwork by the 8th of a month will typically see their hostel deposit refund in that same month's batch, while a student who completes checkout on the 12th will wait until the following month's processing cycle, adding up to four weeks to the effective turnaround time for getting their hostel deposit back. The Accounts Office publishes refund batch processing dates on the student portal at the start of each semester for transparency.


## FEE-6: Payment Methods and Receipts
<!-- section_id: FEE-6 | conflict_group: null -->

The university accepts fee payments through the following channels: online bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS to the university's designated account, with the student's enrollment number as the payment reference), debit or credit card payment through the student portal's integrated payment gateway, demand draft drawn in favor of "Medicaps University" payable at the city in which the main campus is located, and cash payment at the Accounts Office counter (for amounts up to ₹20,000 only, in compliance with institutional cash-handling limits). Regardless of payment method, students must ensure the transaction is reflected in the student portal within 48 hours; if a payment made via bank transfer does not appear on the portal within this window, the student should contact the Accounts Office with the transaction reference number for manual reconciliation. Official fee receipts are generated on the portal and serve as proof of payment for scholarship applications, visa documentation, and other purposes where fee clearance must be demonstrated.
