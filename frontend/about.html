Ask About

The rulebook that argues with itself.

Rulebook QA reads Medicaps University's policy documents, indexes every clause, and answers student questions with a citation to the exact section it came from. Three contradictions have been deliberately planted in the corpus — when a question touches one, the system doesn't guess. It shows you both rules and says so plainly.

How it works

Every question moves through the same four-stage pipeline before an answer reaches the page.

Ingest

Five policy documents are parsed into 40 individually numbered sections.

Index

A TF-IDF index is built over the corpus so a question can be matched to the closest clauses.

Classify

Results are checked in order: conflict, then not-covered, then answered.

Answer

An extractive answer is returned, optionally rephrased by Gemini if an API key is set.

The corpus

Five source files, 40 sections, roughly 7,000 words of policy text.

Three planted contradictions

Real policy handbooks disagree with themselves more often than anyone admits. These are seeded on purpose so the system can prove it catches them.

Attendance threshold

CG-ATTENDANCE · 3-way

"What is the minimum attendance percentage I need to sit my exam?"

ATT-2

75% attendance required.

VS
ATT-5-MEDICAL

60% permitted with a medical certificate — and EXAM-7-COMMITTEE allows a full waiver.

Hostel deposit refund

CG-HOSTEL-REFUND · 2-way

"If I leave the hostel early, when do I get my deposit back?"

HOSTEL-4-REFUND-A

Refund issued within 15 working days.

VS
FEE-5-REFUND-B

No refunds are issued after the 10th of the month.

Scholarship GPA renewal

CG-SCHOLARSHIP-RENEWAL · 2-way

"What GPA do I need to keep my scholarship?"

SCH-4-GPA-A

CGPA of 6.5 or higher, checked annually.

VS
SCH-6-GPA-B

CGPA of 7.0 or higher, checked every semester.
